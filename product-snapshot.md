# Product Snapshot: Content Rules

Renegade Plus entry offers, for the Social Content Manager. This is the content-safe version of the Product Snapshot in the Social Content System Project. Internal pricing has been removed on purpose, because content never states prices. If 01 Standards and Rules in Notion says anything different, Notion wins.

## Rules for all content

- Never state or hint at prices.
- Never pitch The First 30, the Content System Retainer, Signal Strategy, Funnel Build, Funnel Maintenance or any retainer.
- Content may point only to the four entry offers below.
- One offer per post. Pick the strongest fit for the topic.
- Faceless always.

## The four entry offers

### Content Repurposing Sprint
A 10-day, fixed-scope engagement. The client submits their best long-form piece. Renegade Plus builds a custom six-agent AI workflow, runs the first full batch of assets, and hands over the system with a prompt library and production guide. Nothing publishes without client approval.

- Podcast hosts: one episode becomes 2 to 3 short-form clips, 1 carousel, 1 newsletter section and 1 quote graphic.
- Long-form and recorded content: one recording becomes 3 to 5 short-form clips, 2 carousels, 1 newsletter edition and 1 LinkedIn post where it fits.
- The client keeps the workflow, a prompt library in their voice, a VA-ready production guide, the first piece repurposed, and a 30-day calendar template.
- Audience signal: long-form exists but short-form cuts, carousels or a newsletter are missing or inconsistent.

### Content System Build
A production workflow for people who already post but whose output depends on one overloaded person. Ideas, drafts, captions and repurposing run through a system in their voice, and they approve before anything goes out.

- Audience signal: irregular cadence, "need to post more", content burnout, hiring an editor or VA.

### Signal Audit
A diagnostic built on Afiq's seven-step Signal Framework: awareness diagnosis, entry point selection, risk removal, OTO logic, drop-off recovery, nurture sequencing and the direct-response page. It maps where the funnel breaks and what to fix. A brief, not a build.

- Audience signal: content or traffic exists but the path to sale leaks. Bio link to a homepage, no lead magnet, opt-in with no follow-up, webinar with no nurture, "book a call" with nothing in between.

### AI Workflow Systems
AI automations and agent harnesses that take manual conversion and ops work off the owner: DM and enquiry handling, comment-to-DM funnels, lead capture into a CRM, follow-up and nurture, launch admin. The client stays in control of anything customer-facing.

- Audience signal: "comment X and I'll DM you" handled by hand, "DM me for details", enquiry forms with no autoresponder, slow follow-up, no CRM.

## Why it matters to the buyer (context, not copy)

- Most operators still don't repurpose content. The bottleneck is owning the workflow and keeping the voice across formats.
- Buyers form opinions before they reach a website, so funnels built on awareness alone leak. The gap between audience and paying client is structural.
- Manual conversion work caps growth at the owner's hours, and leads answered late convert worse.
- A repurposing workflow pays for itself when it saves 4 to 6 hours a week of manual clipping, writing and scheduling.
